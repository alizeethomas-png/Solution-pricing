Frequently asked questions

What are Flex Credits?

Flex Credits are a flexible way to pay for what you use across Salesforce. This calculator helps you estimate how many you’ll need based on your industry, company size, and the currency you buy in.

Why do you ask for my industry, company size, and currency?

These three inputs set sensible default values and recommendations, so your estimate starts closer to reality. You can adjust everything later — they just give the calculator a better starting point.

Will my estimate be shown in my local currency?

Yes. Choosing a currency up front means your estimate is shown in the currency you actually buy in — no leaving the page to work out conversions yourself.

Is the estimate a binding quote?

No. The calculator gives you a fast, transparent estimate to explore pricing. It isn’t a contract or a final quote — final pricing is confirmed when you’re ready to move forward.

Do I have to talk to sales to get an estimate?

No — the whole point is to let you estimate pricing yourself, first. You can talk to sales whenever you’re ready, with a clearer picture already in hand.
