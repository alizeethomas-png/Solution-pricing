Frequently asked questions

Are there upfront costs or additional fees?

The price shown is for the listed product and edition. Some capabilities, add-ons, or higher usage tiers may carry additional cost — you’ll see those clearly before you buy, and an expert can walk you through a complete quote.

What if my business priorities and product needs have changed?

Your plan can grow and change with you. You can add products, move to a different edition, or adjust as priorities shift — talk to your account team to find the right fit.

Do Salesforce products integrate with outside applications?

Yes. Salesforce connects to thousands of apps through MuleSoft, prebuilt connectors, and the AppExchange marketplace, so you can bring your existing tools and data together.

What support and service packages are available?

Every license includes a standard success plan. Premier and Signature success plans add faster support, expert guidance, and proactive monitoring for teams that need more.

Can I pay monthly?

Monthly billing is available on many products and buying models. Some editions are billed annually — the pricing details for each product note the available terms.

How long are your contracts?

Contract length varies by product and buying model, from month-to-month options to multi-year agreements. Longer commitments can unlock better rates.

Can I upgrade at any time? Can I add more products later?

Yes. You can upgrade editions or add products at any time, and new capabilities are available to you right away.

How does the free trial work?

Free trials let you explore a product with sample data and core features for a set period — no credit card required. You can turn the trial into a paid subscription whenever you’re ready.

Will my data be private and safe?

Trust is our number one value. Your data is protected by robust, independently audited security controls and privacy safeguards — visit the Salesforce Trust site for details.
