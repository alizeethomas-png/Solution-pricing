Buying Models

Buying models built to scale on your terms

Flexible ways to buy Salesforce consumption services, from experimentation to transformation. Choose the buying model that fits your business: pay as you go with no commitment, commit to a baseline for scaled savings, or pre-purchase upfront for the greatest savings.

Choose how you buy

Three ways to buy

Pay-as-you-go

Get started with no upfront payment or usage commitment. Pay only for what you use, billed monthly at list price with no minimums.

Pre-commit

Commit to a baseline of usage with no upfront payment. Savings scale with the size of your commitment.

Pre-purchase

Pay upfront for the greatest savings. Savings scale with the size of your pre-purchased commitment.

How each model works

Comparison Pay-as-you-go Pre-commit Pre-purchase
Best for Customers wanting to experiment or learn usage patterns before committing Customers who want predictable costs with the flexibility to scale Customers with predictable usage who want the greatest savings
Payment timing Monthly, billed in arrears Monthly, billed in arrears Upfront
Minimum commitment None Required — if usage falls below commitment, the remaining balance is invoiced Required — usage draws down from your balance over the contract term

Frequently asked questions

Can I have multiple buying models?

Yes. Buying models are selected per product, giving you the flexibility to choose the model that best fits how each part of your business operates. Work with your account executive to understand what buying model is best for you.

How do overages work?

For pay-go, there are no overages — all usage is charged at list price. For pre-commit and pre-purchased, any overage will be charged at the contracted rate.

Can I move from one buying model to another?

Our models are designed to be flexible and to meet customers where they are in their journey. Work with your account executive to understand what buying model is best for you and more about this process.

Is there a pricing plan tailored for enterprise-wide transformation?

Yes. For organizations ready to scale AI and data across their enterprise, Salesforce offers the Agentic Enterprise License Agreement (AELA): a fixed-price, fixed-term agreement with unlimited access to Salesforce consumption services, no overages, and no usage-based billing. Teams can innovate and expand with full price predictability. To learn more, contact your Salesforce account executive.

Can you give me more information on Flex Credits for Agentforce and Data 360?

Additional information, examples and pricing can be found for Agentforce here and Data 360 here.

Do I have to decide how my Flex Credits will be used when I buy them?

No. Flex Credits can be spent across Agentforce and Data 360 based on where you need them, with no upfront allocation required. As more products move onto Flex Credits, your investment will allow for seamless flexibility.

What tools are available to help me forecast how many Flex Credits I need?

To estimate how many Flex Credits you may need for one or many use cases, check our Flex Credits pricing tool. After purchasing Flex Credits, all usage will be tracked in Digital Wallet with granular usage insights. Learn more here.
